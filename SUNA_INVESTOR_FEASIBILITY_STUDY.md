## 1. Executive Summary & Investment Thesis

SUNA (سونا) is a focused, fast-casual food concept designed to disrupt the fragmented fast-food landscape in Homs, Syria. While the city contains numerous traditional cafeterias and "snack" spots, the local market suffers from menu overextension: restaurants commonly attempt to serve 35–60 items (shawarma, escalope, fried chicken, pizzas, fatayer, generic thick burgers), resulting in severe ingredient spoilage, sluggish service, and mediocre culinary execution.

SUNA adopts the disciplined operational philosophy proven across leading modern concepts in Jeddah and global fast-casual leaders: **relentless focus on just three master-crafted sandwiches**, paired with in-house crafted sauces, curated artisan breads, and rapid-fire assembly line execution.

### The Institutional Reality Check:
* **The Opportunity:** A proven culinary appetite in Homs for modern comfort food, with zero established brands delivering an authentic, crispy-edged smash burger or a Parisian herb-butter entrecôte steak sandwich.
* **The Founder Advantage:** Hasan Oyoun AlSoud combines restaurant operations exposure in Saudi Arabia (Pro House) with recognized digital marketing acumen in Homs (generating over 10 million organic views across local food and business content in 2025). This asset reduces customer acquisition costs during launch by ~70%.
* **The Skeptical Filter:** Content views do not equal customer transactions. The project cannot succeed on social media hype alone. It requires strict unit economics, cold-chain refrigeration integrity, and robust power generation.
* **Capital Reality Check:** The founder's preliminary expectation of launching within **$10,000–$20,000 USD is rejected by this evaluation**. Undercapitalizing on power generation, commercial refrigeration, or working capital reserves creates fatal single-point-of-failure vulnerabilities in Homs. The audited required startup capital is **$24,800 USD**.

## 4. Product Engineering & Culinary Specifications

### A. SUNA Signature Double Smash Burger
* **Patty Engineering:** 2 × 60g beef patties (120g total). 80/20 lean-to-fat ratio utilizing local fresh chuck (رقبة عجل) and brisket/short rib fat. Single 120g patty was rejected because it cannot achieve the required surface-area Maillard caramelization without overcooking the center.
* **Technique:** High-pressure Oklahoma-style smash directly over paper-thin sliced white onions on a 230°C heavy chrome-plated flat-top griddle.
* **Toppings & Assembly:** 2 slices melted American cheddar, house lacto-fermented dill cucumber chips, SUNA amber sauce (tangy mayo, smoked paprika, relish reduction), served in a potato-brioche bun toasted in clarified ghee/butter.
* **Cost & Pricing:** Food Cost: **$1.62 USD (22,400 SYP)** | Packaging: **$0.26 USD** | Selling Price (Sandwich): **$3.60 USD (50,000 SYP)** | Combo: **$4.90 USD (68,000 SYP)** | **Gross Contribution Margin: 55.0%**.

### B. Parisian Entrecôte Sliced Steak Sandwich
* **Cut Selection:** Local fresh beef tenderloin (فيليه) or ribeye (أنتركوت) sliced into thin ribbons across the grain.
* **Concept Chosen:** Concept B (Café de Paris Herb-Butter Emulsion). Seared on high heat for 60 seconds, tossed in warm herb-butter cream sauce (tarragon, parsley, dijon, garlic, anchovy essence), topped with crisp matchstick potato straws (Pommes Allumettes) inside a soft craft sub roll.
* **Cost & Pricing:** Food Cost: **$2.45 USD (33,800 SYP)** | Packaging: **$0.30 USD** | Selling Price (Sandwich): **$5.20 USD (72,000 SYP)** | Combo: **$6.50 USD (90,000 SYP)** | **Gross Contribution Margin: 52.9%**.

### C. SUNA Golden Crispy Chicken Sandwich
* **Cut & Brine:** 140g fresh whole boneless chicken thigh (فخذ دجاج كامل). 24-hour buttermilk and pickle-brine bath to ensure deep moisture retention and tenderness.
* **Breading & Frying:** Double-dredged in seasoned flour, deep-fried at 175°C for 5 minutes.
* **Garnish:** Garlic emulsion (Aioli), crunchy pickled cucumbers, and a crisp honey-cider cabbage slaw in a brioche bun.
* **Cost & Pricing:** Food Cost: **$1.38 USD (19,000 SYP)** | Packaging: **$0.24 USD** | Selling Price (Sandwich): **$3.20 USD (44,000 SYP)** | Combo: **$4.50 USD (62,000 SYP)** | **Gross Contribution Margin: 56.9%**.

## 5. Bread Strategy & Homemade Philosophy

> [!CRITICAL]
> **Operational Directive: Zero Bread Baking Inside the Food Truck**
> Attempting to mix, ferment, proof, and bake brioche buns inside a 4.5m food truck creates unacceptable operating conditions:
> 1. Airborne flour dust poses severe fire and contamination hazards near deep fat fryers.
> 2. Proofing cabinets and commercial convection ovens occupy over 1.8 cubic meters of critical space.
> 3. Heat dissipation raises kitchen cabin temperatures past 45°C, degrading staff productivity and food safety.

### The Staged Solution:
* **Launch Phase:** Contract an established artisan bakery in Homs under an exclusive recipe licensing agreement with non-disclosure protections. Buns are baked fresh every morning and delivered in aerated crates at 11:00 AM and 5:00 PM.
* **Bread Unit Cost:** Estimated at **$0.22 USD (3,000 SYP)** per brioche bun and **$0.28 USD (3,800 SYP)** per sub roll—significantly cheaper than maintaining internal baking equipment and dedicated bakery labor in Month 1.
* **Phase 2 (Year 2):** Transition bread production to a dedicated low-cost off-site central commissary (Dark Prep Hub).

### In-House vs. Outsourced Matrix:
* **Made In-House Daily:** Sauces (SUNA Amber, Café de Paris, Garlic Aioli), meat portioning and patty smashing, chicken brining, pickled cucumbers, and French fry seasoning.
* **Outsourced to Certified Partners:** Brioche buns & sub baguettes, raw butchery cuts (delivered pre-trimmed daily), pasteurized dairy products (cheddar cheese slices, butter), and branded packaging manufacturing.

## 9. Break-Even Analysis & Daily Sales Model

### Daily Operational Break-Even:
* **Fixed Overhead Burden per Day:** $1,900 / 30 = **$63.33 USD / day**
* **Weighted Contribution Margin per Order:**
  $$\text{Average Order Value} (\$5.20) - \text{Food Cost} (\$1.87) - \text{Packaging} (\$0.28) - \text{Delivery Impact} (\$0.22) = \mathbf{\$2.83\text{ USD}}$$
* **Break-Even Orders per Day:**
  $$\frac{\$63.33}{\$2.83} = \mathbf{22.4\text{ orders/day (to cover operating overhead only)}}$$
* **Break-Even Including Capex Amortization over 12 Months ($2,067/mo):**
  $$\frac{\$63.33 + \$68.90}{\$2.83} = \mathbf{46.7\text{ orders/day}}$$
* **Margin of Safety:** In the Base Case (95 orders/day), SUNA operates at **103% above operating break-even**, offering exceptional downside insulation against sales fluctuations.
